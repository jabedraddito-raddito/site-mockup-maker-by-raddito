HOW TO USE

Double-click "Site Mockup Maker" on your Desktop. A window opens:
  1. Paste your website links in the big box, one per line.
  2. Pick the mockup size.
  3. Tick the styles you want.
  4. Press "Make Mockups" and wait for "Done".
Then click "Open finished mockups". Each website has its own folder in Desktop\Mockups.
